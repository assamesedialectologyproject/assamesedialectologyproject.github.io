Corpus of Contemporary Assamese (2023–)

The audio corpus is archived with PARADISEC (Pacific and Regional Archive for Digital Sources in Endangered Cultures). The audio files are currently under closed access.

Transcriptions may be made available upon request. To enquire about access, email saurabhkumarnath@gmail.com with the subject line COCAS access.
